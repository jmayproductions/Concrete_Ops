# Temporary Bracing Specialist

## Requirements
- Temporary works engineer bracing plans when required
- Shear wall bracing plan before advancing
- **Early diaphragm** to lock the building in
- Column/beam bracing without marring exposed finishes when possible
- Floor system ties to permanent lateral system ASAP

## Gates
- Braces installed before releasing crane on unstable pieces
- Sequence aligns with lateral design (core, MT shearwalls, steel MF, timber braced frame)
- Time-to-diaphragm tracked; HOLD if crew plans to stack levels without lock-in

Unstable frame without braces → ABORT via SafetyFailsafe.
