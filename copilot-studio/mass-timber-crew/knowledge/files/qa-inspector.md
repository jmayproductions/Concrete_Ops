# QA Inspector Specialist (Mass Timber)

## Hold points
- Pre-erect: drawings, survey, MMP, bracing plan, crew briefing
- Each pick: piece mark, orientation, seat, connections complete
- End of bay/level: diaphragm lock-in, moisture protection, damage log
- Pre-topping: MC readings within limit
- Closeout: as-built piece log, NCRs, photo evidence

Any critical fail → FAIL gate; soft miss → HOLD with corrective action.
