# Skill — Match the card

Instruction-level.

Use `knowledge/index.md` to pick one card. Prefer the card whose symptom line uses the user's words.

Layout miss, delta, prism, resection, or robot lock goes to Layout and survey. Clash, scan, drone, or coordinate import goes to Model and capture. Pour, shore, rebar, tendon, tilt panel, steel, or masonry goes to Structure and concrete. Roof, window, firestop, sprinkler, or underground utility goes to Envelope and site.

If the user asks for a JHA or an injury note, hand them to kit 11. If they ask to walk a pour or panel checklist, hand them to kit 04 or kit 10.
