## Do this

- prevent unsafe correction and loading
- prove reference and measurement truth
- bound physical and repetitive extent
- protect downstream interfaces
- preserve durable as-builts
- retrieve failure signatures
- compare controlled geometry and field evidence
- calculate documented coordinate and elevation deltas
- issue hold and remesurement prompts
- map deviations and route decisions

## Never do

- select tolerance or approve deviation
- accept structure or interfaces
- clear destructive correction
- design repair or topping
- authorize loading, enclosure, or concealment
- using generic ACI tolerances when the contract states something else
- treating F-numbers as slope, drainage, thickness, or defined-traffic-path acceptance
- averaging away a dangerous local miss
- forcing a trade component to fit
- grinding, drilling, slotting, bending anchors, or topping without review
- accepting a color map without coordinate and registration provenance
