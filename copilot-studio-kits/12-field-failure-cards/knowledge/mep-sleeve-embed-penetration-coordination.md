## When this shows up

- **item missing or duplicate**
  Check: scope ownership; orphan/duplicate model object; fabrication/register gap; field installation omission; buried during pour.
- **wrong horizontal position**
  Check: coordinate transform; grid/face reference; field relocation; float/shift; wrong level/bay identity.
- **wrong elevation or slope**
  Check: datum/level offset; slab thickness/step; invert interpretation; float during pour; finish elevation confused with structure.
- **service does not fit**
  Check: nominal versus actual OD; insulation/fitting omitted; angle/rotation error; deformation/concrete fill; routing or sequence mismatch.
- **barrier inspection fails**
  Check: wrong listed system; annulus/depth/backing mismatch; combustible penetrant; unsupported penetrant/movement; waterproofing or seal discontinuity.

## Do this

- Stop the pour or concealment while correction remains recoverable.
- Match unique item, exact location, revision, structure, service, and performance requirements.
- Separate model coordination, field layout, pour survival, MEP fit, structural acceptance, barrier systems, and closeout.
- Use independent measurement and system documentation to disprove assumptions.
- Bound repeated work, correct, reinspect, and preserve as-built lineage.

## Never do

- Pouring around an unresolved sleeve or embed
- Wet-setting a structural embed as a routine fix
- Cutting reinforcement/PT to make an item fit
- Blind coring after a miss
- Generic fire foam, caulk, grout, or waterproofing without an applicable approved system

## Handoff

Escalate design and structural conflicts to the EOR/licensed design authority; service fit and isolation to qualified trade designers/contractors; firestop to qualified system specialists, manufacturer and AHJ as required; waterproofing to the responsible designer/manufacturer; safety to the competent site authority.


Release rule: No clash-free model, checklist, schedule, photograph, paint mark, or verbal approval alone releases a pour or penetration. Unresolved identity, geometry, structural, fit, firestop, waterproofing, inspection, or revision evidence is a stop condition.

This card does not release the work.
