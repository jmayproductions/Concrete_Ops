## Do this

- worker safety
- stop bad material entering work
- preserve time-sensitive evidence
- protect fresh concrete
- bound latent defects
- retrieve likely failure modes
- compare time-stamped records
- ask discriminating field questions
- map affected limits and create holds
- route to authorized decision makers

## Never do

- mixture acceptance
- water or admixture authorization
- structural acceptance
- repair design
- form-removal or loading release
- adding unrecorded water or admixture
- placing a rejected or unidentified load
- continuing through an unplanned stop without a joint decision
- patching honeycomb before bounding depth
- finishing bleed water into the surface
- ending curing early for schedule
- accepting concrete from appearance or one cylinder alone
