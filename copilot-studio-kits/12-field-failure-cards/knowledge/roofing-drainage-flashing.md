## Do this

- protect people above and below the roof
- preserve separate primary and emergency drainage
- stop loading, concealment, hot work, and improvised repairs
- prove assembly, basin, temporary state, and hazard identity
- protect testing, warranty, maintenance, and turnover evidence
- retrieve roof failure signatures
- reconcile roof, drainage, material, test, warranty, and as-built evidence
- calculate documented slopes, elevations, depths, and quantity differences
- issue holds and roof or below-roof exclusion prompts
- map basins, wet zones, leak paths, and repeated conditions and route decisions

## Never do

- assess structural stability or rain and snow capacity
- size or alter drainage or direct pond removal
- design wind attachment or select roof materials and repairs
- authorize roof access, fall protection, hot work, flood tests, or penetrations
- release warranty, concealment, occupancy, or roof acceptance
- treating no interior leak as proof that ponding and rain loads are safe
- blocking or altering overflow drainage to stop visible water
- pumping or releasing a large roof pond without structural and drainage direction
- flood testing without verified structural capacity, containment, drainage, and emergency plan
- patching over wet insulation or unidentified leak travel
- accepting seams without required calibration, probing, sampling, and repair verification
- allowing hot work without substrate and concealed-combustible review and fire watch
- closing the roof before drains, curbs, penetrations, edges, and temporary tie-ins are inspected
