## When this shows up

- **no response**
  Check: ticket/ownership gap; nonmetallic or broken tracer; passive signal absent; GPR attenuation; access/coverage gap.
- **too many or wandering responses**
  Check: bonding/bleed; high frequency; induction air coupling; parallel conductors; overload.
- **depth inconsistent**
  Check: distorted EM field; wrong GPR velocity; sonde reference/pitch; coordinate/datum error; record depth unreliable.
- **records and field disagree**
  Check: quality-level mismatch; relocation/abandonment; wrong utility identity; coordinate transform; incomplete investigation.
- **clearance fails at excavation**
  Check: paint treated exact; facility width omitted; bend/transition between checks; wrong pothole location; excavation swept volume omitted.

## Do this

- Stop and protect people/facilities first.
- Verify ticket, scope, ownership, responses, and jurisdiction.
- Separate record quality, detection evidence, exact exposure, survey capture, and digital-model release.
- Use the fastest independent test that can disprove the current interpretation.
- Bound affected work and escalate to the proper utility/SUE/survey/safety/design authority.

## Never do

- Digging from a clear passive sweep
- Assigning utility identity from color or one anomaly
- Reporting instrument depth as exact cover
- Dowsing or blind probing
- Concealing contact or backfilling before owner inspection

## Handoff

Escalate ticket/mark/ownership to the one-call center and utility operator; detection uncertainty to qualified locator/SUE/geophysics personnel; exact position and coordinates to qualified survey/SUE authority; excavation safety to the competent person; damage/release to emergency services and utility owner as required.


Release rule: No single mark, signal, anomaly, record, depth reading, or digital model authorizes excavation or proves clearance. Consequential conflicts require converging evidence and safe exact-location determination under the governing authority.

This card does not release the work.
