## When this shows up

- **fresh concrete out of tolerance**
  Check: wrong mix/ticket; water or admixture addition; age/temperature; sampling/test error; producer variability.
- **cold joint or honeycomb**
  Check: truck gap; lift discontinuity; vibrator access/failure; segregation/drop; form leakage/congestion.
- **early surface cracking or delamination**
  Check: evaporation/plastic shrinkage; bleed-water finishing; water addition; late joints; curing/weather/freeze.
- **geometry or FF/FL failure**
  Check: control/screed setup; form movement; finish timing; local handwork; test timing/method.
- **low or erratic strength results**
  Check: representativeness; cylinder fabrication/curing; mix/addition deviation; lab/ID error; actual in-place deficiency.

## Do this

- Protect workers and stop recoverable placement damage first.
- Identify exact mix/truck/time/location and physical conditions.
- Separate fresh-property testing, placement workmanship, hardened defects, specimen validity, in-place performance, and structural acceptance.
- Use independent evidence capable of disproving the favored explanation.
- Bound affected concrete, preserve records, escalate, evaluate, repair, verify, and prevent recurrence.

## Never do

- Unrecorded water or additives
- Finishing bleed/rain water into concrete
- Accepting honeycomb or low breaks from appearance
- Stripping/loading from calendar age
- Cosmetic patching before defect mapping and disposition

## Handoff

Escalate mix/batch issues to producer technical services and testing authority; workmanship/weather/curing to qualified concrete supervision and inspection; strength, joints, defects, repair, stripping, stressing, and loads to the EOR/licensed design authority; worker hazards to the competent safety authority.


Release rule: No appearance, calendar age, verbal assurance, slump, single cylinder, patch, or maturity estimate alone authorizes structural acceptance or loading. Unresolved material history, testing validity, defect scope, curing, strength, or authority is a stop condition.
