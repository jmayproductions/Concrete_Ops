## When this shows up

- **nothing detected**
  Check: coverage/contact gap; attenuation or depth limit; weak/nonmetallic target; orientation/resolution; wrong scanner mode or face.
- **many or ambiguous targets**
  Check: mesh/rebar congestion; PT/rebar misclassification; multiple reflection or deck; closely spaced conduits; processing artifact.
- **depth or mark disagreement**
  Check: dielectric/calibration error; wrong face/mirror transfer; unstable reference; target drape or inclination; drawing/as-built mismatch.
- **unexpected resistance, twang, spark, water, void, or distress**
  Check: reinforcement/PT contact; live conduit or pipe; structural steel/deck; exit-face breakout; preexisting damage.
- **permit and field mismatch**
  Check: design revision; location/diameter drift; wrong level/bay/face; report-mark separation; unauthorized field relocation.

## Do this

- Stop cutting and protect both faces first.
- Match exact permit geometry to the physical slab and identify the structural system.
- Separate detection confidence, target identity, structural authorization, service isolation, and execution controls.
- Use an independent test capable of disproving the current interpretation.
- Bound dependent work, preserve evidence, escalate, repair, verify, and document before release.

## Never do

- Blind drilling or cutting
- Treating no-response as clear
- Cutting a bar or tendon to make the hole work
- Guessing target identity or depth
- Concealing a nick, strike, crack, or near miss

## Handoff

Escalate structural capacity, PT/prestress, reinforcement, steel/deck, openings, and repairs to the engineer of record or qualified licensed design professional; scan/radiography limits to qualified specialists; energized/pressurized systems to their owners and qualified trades; worker hazards to the competent safety authority.


Release rule: No single scan, image, no-response, drawing, mark, or scanner statement proves clearance or authorizes drilling. Unresolved ambiguity at a consequential location is a stop condition.
