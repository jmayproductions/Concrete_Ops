## Never do

- direct lifting or rigging
- approve temporary stability or brace removal
- accept bearings or connections
- design field modifications or repairs
- release loading, topping, work below, or structural acceptance
- lifting from generic weight or assumed center of gravity
- using an unverified insert or improvised rigging point
- standing beneath or inside a member's fall or overturn zone
- releasing the crane because a member appears seated
- counting incomplete welds, loose bolts, shims, or unset grout as permanent restraint
- removing braces from schedule pressure or verbal direction
- field cutting or forced fit-up before strand, rebar, insert, and load-path review
