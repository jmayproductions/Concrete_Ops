## Never do

- design framing, backing, ceiling support, or seismic restraint
- approve a tested assembly, firestop, joint, shaft, or membrane
- certify accessibility, acoustic, fire, smoke, structural, or load performance
- clear work above an unsafe ceiling or select repair
- release concealment, occupancy, or final acceptance
- substituting a similar-looking product or assembly without documented approval
- hard-fastening a slip or deflection detail or filling its movement zone
- hanging services, partitions, signs, or access loads from an unapproved ceiling grid
- closing a rated wall, shaft, backing zone, or ceiling before required inspection
- accepting fire and smoke continuity from finish appearance alone
- using ordinary gypsum board where wet-area or listed products are required
- patching or painting before defect extent and cause are documented
- walking or crawling on suspended ceilings not designed as work platforms
