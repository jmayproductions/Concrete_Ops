## Do this

- clear fall and collapse zones
- preserve current-stage stability
- prove piece, joint, and revision identity
- bound repeated conditions
- protect inspection and turnover evidence
- retrieve failure signatures
- reconcile piece, bolt, weld, survey, and inspection evidence
- calculate documented geometry differences
- issue exclusion-zone and hold prompts
- map repeated conditions and route decisions

## Never do

- direct crane or erection operations
- approve temporary stability
- accept bolts, welds, NDE, or connections
- design field modifications or repairs
- release loading, decking, fireproofing, concealment, or structural acceptance
- erecting without written concrete-strength and anchor readiness
- releasing a crane onto an incomplete or unverified connection
- forcing fit with crane side load, come-alongs, heat, impacts, or unauthorized hole changes
- mixing bolt lots or accepting bolts by appearance or torque alone
- welding outside an approved WPS or before fit-up and preheat control
- landing bundles on unsecured joists or deck
- concealing steel before required inspection and records
