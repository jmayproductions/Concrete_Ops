## When this shows up

- **shore leaning, buckling, or settling**
  Check: weak base/sole; overextension or damage; unbraced/eccentric load; spacing/load error; supporting slab/ground failure.
- **wall or column form bowing/leaking/popping**
  Check: pressure/pour rate; tie/clamp/waler failure; brace/anchor movement; joint/bulkhead weakness; vibration or asymmetric fill.
- **deck elevation or deflection wrong**
  Check: control/camber; joist/stringer span; shore settlement; unstacked load path; construction overload.
- **collapse after stripping or upper-floor work**
  Check: low in-place strength; shore-to-reshore gap; incorrect reshore force/spacing; unplanned construction load; multi-floor load-distribution error.
- **plan and field disagree**
  Check: obsolete revision; component substitution; access-driven removal; unapproved field change; inspection/identity failure.

## Do this

- Stop loading and protect the collapse zone first.
- Trace the actual load path and current engineered plan.
- Separate component capacity, system stability, supporting structure, pour pressure, concrete strength, and construction sequence.
- Use measured field evidence to disprove assumptions.
- Bound repeats, engage authorities, stabilize/correct under design, verify, and document release.

## Never do

- Rule-of-thumb shore spacing or pour rate
- Random shores under distress
- Adjusting loaded jacks without design
- Stripping from age/appearance
- Reentry beneath buckled or moving work

## Handoff

Escalate temporary-works design/system changes to the qualified formwork engineer; permanent-structure strength/load/deflection to the EOR; system use to manufacturer/supplier technical authority; inspection/emergency/exclusion to the competent safety authority; evidence preservation to project incident leadership.


Release rule: No generic detail, component label, calendar age, visual hardness, single cylinder, extra shore, verbal direction, or successful prior floor alone authorizes pour, adjustment, stripping, loading, or reentry.

This card does not release the work.
