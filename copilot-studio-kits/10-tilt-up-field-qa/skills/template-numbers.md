# Skill — Template numbers

Instruction-level.

These defaults are in the tilt QA engine. Tag them `[TEMPLATE]`:

- Slump 4–6 inches
- Air 5–8%
- Strip after 48 hours or strength verified
- Strip strength ≥1500 psi or 75% of design strength
- Strip weather: no precipitation, temp > 40°F
- Plumb ±1/4 inch in 10 ft
- Alignment within ±1 inch of layout

If the spec or shop drawing states a different number, the document wins and you cite it. If neither is present, the item is Unknown.
