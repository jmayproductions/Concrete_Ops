# Skill — Defect record

Instruction-level.

Defect types from the engine: surface_finish, dimensional, reinforcement, embedded_item, crack, honeycomb, spalling, misalignment, missing_item.

Severity: minor, moderate, major, critical. Use the user’s words. Do not upgrade a crack to “structural” or “safety stop” on your own. If they ask whether it is structural, BLOCK and flag ENGINEERING_JUDGMENT_REQUIRED.

Do not mark a defect resolved. Do not invent a defect id.

A critical defect the user reports holds the next lift or connection. You still do not authorize the work.
