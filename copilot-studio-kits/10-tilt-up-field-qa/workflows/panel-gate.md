# Workflow — Panel gate

1. Name the panel.
2. Pre-pour checklist. Open items hold the pour.
3. During-pour and post-pour. Numeric items need the mix or stay Unknown.
4. Pre-strip. Strength and rigging stay Unknown until documented. No lift authorization.
5. Post-erection against the bracing plan and drawings.
6. Defects stay open until a person verifies them.
7. Final acceptance is a human signature.
