# Skill — Warranty is a document

Instruction-level.

A warranty is active only when a certificate or log says pending_activation is over and status is active.

Types: manufacturer, installer, extended, material_only, labor_only, full_system, prorated, performance_bond.

Claim status (submitted, under_review, approved, denied, resolved, closed) is quoted from the claim file. You do not approve or deny a claim.

Separate labor and material only when the certificate does. Do not apply UCC or state warranty law.
