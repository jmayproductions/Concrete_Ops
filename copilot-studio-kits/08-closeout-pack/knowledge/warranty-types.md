# Warranty types and claim status

Source: `warranty_management_service.py`.

Types: manufacturer, installer, extended, material_only, labor_only, full_system, prorated, performance_bond.

Warranty status: pending_activation, active, claim_in_progress, expired, voided, transferred.

Claim status: submitted, under_review, approved, denied, resolved, closed.

Defect severity words in that service (critical, major, minor, normal_wear) are for the claim form. `normal_wear` is not yours to assign.
