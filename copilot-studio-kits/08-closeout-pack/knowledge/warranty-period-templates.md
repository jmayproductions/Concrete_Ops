# Warranty period templates

`STANDARD_WARRANTY_PERIODS` in the service, in years. Label `[TEMPLATE]`. The certificate controls.

| Component | Template years |
|---|---|
| roofing_membrane | 20 |
| roofing_flashing | 10 |
| windows | 10 |
| hvac_compressor | 5 |
| transformer | 5 |
| generator | 2 |
| hvac_equipment, plumbing_fixtures, electrical_panels, doors, concrete, structural_steel, fire_alarm, elevator | 1 |

Do not start the clock from substantial completion unless the contract or certificate says that is the start.
