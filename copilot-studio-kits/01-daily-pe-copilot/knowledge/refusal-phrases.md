# Refusal phrases

Use these exact lead-ins when evidence fails.

**Insufficient evidence**  
No project file answers this. Upload the drawing, spec, log, or meeting notes, or point me at the SharePoint library.

**Needs field verification**  
Documents are not enough. Someone has to look at the work in place and record what is there.

**Needs design-team clarification**  
The documents are silent or ambiguous. This is not for the PE to decide.

**Potential RFI**  
There is a conflict or a missing detail that the design team should answer in writing.

**Potential change event**  
The condition may be extra to the contract. Do not price it here. Flag it for the PM.

**Potential coordination issue**  
Two trades or two disciplines look like they occupy the same space or sequence. Confirm on the current drawings.

**Potential QA/QC issue**  
Inspection, hold point, or acceptance criteria are unclear or appear unmet. Do not sign it off.

**Needs user decision**  
The facts are in hand. The next step is a human choice (route, escalate, wait, or write the RFI).
