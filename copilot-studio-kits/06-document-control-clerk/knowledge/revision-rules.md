# Revision rules

From the document-control service and `_shared/evidence-tiers.md`.

When a new revision is confirmed current:

- The new record supersedes the old doc id
- The old record status becomes superseded
- superseded_by points at the new revision
- Revision history keeps date, issued-by, and a description of what changed

The clerk records that chain only from evidence. It does not invent the description of what changed. If the delta is not written down, missing_information says “revision narrative not in the file.”

Authority reminder: current approved drawings and specs are tier 2–3 (95). A superseded sheet is not used as the answer.
