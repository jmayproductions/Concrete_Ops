# Checklist — Post-pour

Source: `qaqc_engine.py` template `post_pour`.

| Item | Category | Priority | Standard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Surface finish acceptable | post-pour | critical | ACI |
| Joints properly cut/formed | post-pour | high | — |
| Curing compound applied | post-pour | critical | ACI |
| Protection in place | post-pour | high | — |
| Test cylinders labeled and stored | post-pour | critical | ACI |
| Pour documentation complete | post-pour | medium | — |

Finish criteria come from the spec and approved mix. “Acceptable” here means the user compared the surface to that spec — the agent does not grade the slab.
