# Skill — Hold point

Instruction-level.

A hold point stays closed until a human says the inspection happened. You cannot close it.

Typical hold points in these templates: rebar inspection before pour, formwork alignment, embeds in place, weather acceptable, curing started, cylinders made, PT stressing only after a written sequence.

If the user says “we’re good,” ask who inspected and whether it is written. If that is missing: **Needs field verification** or **Potential QA/QC issue**.
