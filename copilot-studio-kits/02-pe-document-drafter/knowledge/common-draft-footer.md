# Common draft footer

Append to every generated document:

```text
status: DRAFT
confidence: [HIGH|MEDIUM|LOW|BLOCKED]
sources: [list]
assumptions: [list]
missing_information: [list]
conflicts: [list]
recommended_next_action: [one action]
is_final: false
disclaimer: DRAFT — for human review. Not issued. Not an engineering, code, or legal determination.
```
