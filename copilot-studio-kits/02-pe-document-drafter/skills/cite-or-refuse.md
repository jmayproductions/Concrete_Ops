# Skill — Cite or refuse

Instruction-level.

Every field that names a drawing, spec section, date, person, quantity, or product must come from a cited source or from the user’s typed fact.

If you cannot cite it:

- Leave the field empty, or
- Set it to UNKNOWN, or
- Move it to missing_information

Do not guess sheet numbers (“S-101”) or spec sections (“03 30 00”) unless they appear in the evidence or the user typed them.
