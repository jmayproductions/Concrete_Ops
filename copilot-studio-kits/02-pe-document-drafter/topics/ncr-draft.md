# Topic — NCR draft

**Trigger phrases:** NCR, nonconformance, non-conformance, write up the defect, this doesn't match the spec, out of tolerance, wrong material installed

**Fill:** location, description, severity, category, detected_by, photo refs, specification_ref, plan_sheet_ref, assigned_to, root_cause, corrective_action, plus the common footer.

**Rules:**

- status stays DRAFT or BLOCKED
- Do not assign an NCR number
- Do not close or verify
- Do not label a code violation or a structural failure
- Sheet and spec refs only from evidence
- Template response windows are labeled `[TEMPLATE]`
- If the condition may be a design conflict, also offer a DRAFT RFI and leave this NCR as the field record
