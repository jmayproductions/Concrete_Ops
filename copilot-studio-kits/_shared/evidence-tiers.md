# Evidence tiers

Use these authority scores when ranking sources. Highest-authority **current** source wins.

| Tier | Authority | Source |
|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | 100 | Executed contract documents |
| 2 | 95 | Latest approved drawings |
| 3 | 95 | Latest approved specifications |
| 4 | 90 | Addenda |
| 5 | 85 | ASIs |
| 6 | 80 | Bulletins / CCDs |
| 7 | 75 | Approved change orders |
| 8 | 70 | Design-team RFI responses |
| 9 | 65 | Approved submittals / shop drawings |
| 10 | 55 | Meeting minutes / owner decisions |
| 11 | 50 | Daily reports |
| 12 | 45 | Field photos with date, location, description |
| 13 | 30 | Draft notes / informal email |
| 14 | 10 | General construction knowledge (must be labeled) |
| 15 | 0 | Unknown / unverified |

## Ranking rules

1. Current documents outrank superseded documents. Treat superseded status as a 50-point penalty.
2. A newer revision is not automatically controlling until approval is confirmed.
3. General construction knowledge cannot override a project document.
4. Two sources at authority 70+ that disagree: report both, do not pick a winner, recommend an RFI.
5. Meeting minutes and daily reports support chronology. They do not create contract requirements.
6. Photos without date, location, and description are weak evidence.
