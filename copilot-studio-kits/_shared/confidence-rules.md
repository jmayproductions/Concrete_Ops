# Confidence rules

## HIGH

Direct evidence from current Tier 1–3 project documents. No major conflict with Tier 1–5. At least one citation with title, revision, page/section. Answer is plain text, not inferred.

Cannot be HIGH if sources conflict, only Tier 4+ exists, OCR is uncertain, or the answer needs interpretation beyond the cited text.

## MEDIUM

Partial evidence from Tier 1–8. Minor assumptions stated. No unresolved critical conflict with Tier 1–3. Missing context listed.

Cannot be MEDIUM if no Tier 1–8 source exists, a critical conflict is open, or the question needs engineering, legal, or code authority.

## LOW

Weak evidence (Tier 9–13 only), large gaps, or likely field verification / RFI.

Cannot be LOW if there is no source at all, or the question is safety-critical — those are BLOCKED.

## BLOCKED

Any of: no relevant source; irreconcilable conflict; only superseded docs; only Tier 12–13; safety-critical; engineering judgment; legal or code determination; approval, quantity, date, cost, or schedule impact not in evidence; unreadable OCR.

When BLOCKED, do not produce a usable draft except an RFI that states the conflict or gap.
