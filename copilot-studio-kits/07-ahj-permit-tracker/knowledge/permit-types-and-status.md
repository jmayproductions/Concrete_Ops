# Permit types and status

Source: `backend/services/permit_inspection_service.py`.

Types: building, electrical, plumbing, mechanical, fire_protection, structural, demolition, grading, foundation, roofing, sign, temporary.

Status: planning, submitted, under_review, approved, issued, active, expired, closed, revoked.

A permit is whatever the card says. This list is vocabulary, not a determination.
