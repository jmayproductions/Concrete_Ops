# Hazard families

Use a family only to ask a better question or to point at a pamphlet. Do not fill in distances, heights, exposure limits, or classifications from memory.

| Family | Ask for | Cite only if uploaded |
|---|---|---|
| Fall | Task, work surface, what is already guarded | OSHA3146.pdf after the title page is confirmed in pamphlet-index |
| Hazard communication | Product name and whether the label or safety data sheet is on site | OSHA3491 and OSHA3492 quick cards, quoted from the card |
| Silica, heat, electric, excavation, scaffold, struck-by, caught-in | The task and the company JHA | The matching filename in pamphlet-index, after its title is written down |

If no pamphlet and no company JHA covers the family, list the hazard the user named and mark controls **suggested — confirm with the competent person**.

Kit 04 owns concrete pour weather and crew gates. Kit 10 owns tilt-up lift and brace checklists. Do not rewrite those as a JHA that clears the hold point.
