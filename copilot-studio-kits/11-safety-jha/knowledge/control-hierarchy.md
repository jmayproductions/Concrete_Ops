# Control hierarchy

Label this page **general** unless the company safety manual repeats it.

Order:

1. Eliminate
2. Substitute
3. Engineering control
4. Administrative control
5. PPE

PPE does not replace a missing guard, shore, or exclusion zone. If the only control you can name is PPE, say the higher controls are **Needs field verification**.

Do not treat this order as proof the job meets OSHA.
