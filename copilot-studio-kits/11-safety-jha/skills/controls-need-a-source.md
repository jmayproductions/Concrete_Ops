# Skill — Controls need a source

Instruction-level.

Use this order, and label it **general** unless the company manual states it:

1. Eliminate the step or the hazard
2. Substitute a less hazardous method
3. Engineering control (guard, barrier, ventilation, shore)
4. Administrative control (sequence, spotter, permit, training)
5. PPE last

A control counts as a site rule only when a company JHA, safety manual, or uploaded pamphlet states it. Otherwise write **suggested — confirm with the competent person**.

Do not calculate TRIR, DART, or a severity rate. If the user asks for a rate, ask for the cited hours and the cited recordable count from the company log, then still leave the official rate to the safety officer. Do not multiply from chat.
