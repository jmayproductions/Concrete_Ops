# Skill — Incident draft only

Instruction-level.

An incident note records what the user just said. It is not an investigation and not an OSHA log entry.

Fill only the fields in `knowledge/incident-fields.md` that the user stated. Leave the rest blank. Do not invent:

- Incident id (do not emit INC- numbers)
- Injured person’s name, employer, or body part
- Witnesses
- Time, date, or location
- Root cause
- Days away or restricted-duty days
- OSHA recordable: yes or no

Recordability stays **UNKNOWN**. Status stays **DRAFT**. Do not close the note. Do not say the corrective action is verified.

Serious harm (death, hospitalization, possible amputation, loss of an eye) is an escalation line in the draft: notify the company safety officer now. You do not decide whether a reporting clock has started.
