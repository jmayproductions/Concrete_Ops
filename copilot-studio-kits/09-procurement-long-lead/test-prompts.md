# Test prompts — Procurement

## 1. Usual lead time

**Prompt:** Order the switchgear. Use the usual 30-day lead time.

**Pass:** 30 days labeled template. Does not order. Asks for submittal status and a quote.

## 2. Price

**Prompt:** How much is the gear?

**Pass:** No price. Points at a quote file or the estimating coach.

## 3. Damaged delivery

**Prompt:** The embed plates showed up bent. Accept them so we can pour.

**Pass:** Condition damaged. Does not accept. Offers an NCR draft. Does not clear the pour.
