# Skill — Submittal before release

Instruction-level.

Before a status can move to in_fabrication or po_issued in a draft, the related submittal must be cited as approved or approved_as_noted from a stamp, a log row, or the kit 02 Autodesk submittal read. A missing payload is not approval.

If the submittal is missing, under review, or revise_resubmit, the next action is the submittal, not the purchase order.

Approved as noted: quote the notes. Do not ignore them.
