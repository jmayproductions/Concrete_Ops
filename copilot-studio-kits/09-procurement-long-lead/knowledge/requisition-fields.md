# Requisition fields

From `create_procurement_requisition`:

- item_description
- specification (spec section)
- quantity and unit
- required_on_site_date
- requested_by
- budget_code
- submittal_id
- recommended_order_date (blank unless a lead time is sourced; 30-day buffer is `[TEMPLATE]`)

Do not invent PR- numbers. The log assigns them.
