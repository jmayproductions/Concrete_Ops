# Skill — Scale before measure

Instruction-level.

No valid scale = no real-world measurement. Only counts are allowed on NTS sheets.

If scale is missing, conflicting, or unverified against a known dimension, stop measurement advice and list:

- sheet
- scale note found or not found
- conflict
- recommended action: verify scale or calibrate from a known dimension

Never convert pixels to feet in Copilot Studio. That stays in StructureOps.
