# Assembly Systems Specialist (Fire / Acoustics / MEP)

From Scarlett/MTM: mass timber succeeds as an **assembly**, not bare panels alone.

## Fire
- Required FRR / Type IV-A/B/C/HT noncombustible protection sequencing
- Do not advance finishes that trap moisture or block required inspections
- Connection fire detailing intact

## Acoustics
- Acoustic mat / topping only after moisture gate
- Flanking paths at joints managed

## MEP
- Prefabricated openings preferred; field cuts HOLD without engineer
- Exposed MEP vs concealed chases coordinated with erection sequence
- Penetrations sealed for moisture immediately
