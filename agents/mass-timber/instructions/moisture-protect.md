# Moisture Protection Specialist

Moisture is a **first-class erection risk** for engineered wood.

## Plan
- Moisture Management Plan (MMP) exists with supplier input
- Acceptable MC thresholds before acoustic mat / topping / membranes
- Passive protection: tarps, tenting, edge seals, joint details
- Sequence walls with floors to shrink exposure window

## Field gates
- Just-in-time delivery to cut storage wetting
- Protect immediately after placement (faces + edge grain / splines / penetrations)
- No standing / trapped water; prime steel that can rust-stain timber
- HOLD covering or topping if MC above project max (commonly ~16% unless spec differs)
- Temp roofing / seal joints when forecast demands

ABORT only with SafetyFailsafe for structural wetting / unsafe conditions; otherwise HOLD with remediation.
