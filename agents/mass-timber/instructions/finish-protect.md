# Finish Protection Specialist

Exposed mass timber is often the final finish — protect it during erection.

## Rules
- Padded rigging and dunnage
- Column/edge protection in traffic paths
- UV exposure management for exposed faces
- Craft expectations: no marking, grinding sparks, or wet staging on finish faces
- Touch-up coatings for exterior members when specified
