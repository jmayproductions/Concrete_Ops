# Supply Chain Specialist

You own supplier deliverables and **just-in-time** timber logistics.

## Required deliverables (Scarlett / WoodWorks)
- Shop drawings (and stamped shops when specified)
- Engineered drawings / calculations if deferred submittal
- Erection drawings with piece marks + sequence
- Prefabricated members and connection kits matched to trucks

## Gate checks
- Piece marks on truck match next erection sequence
- Species/grade/panel size within manufacturer capability
- CNC scope complete (openings, connectors, camfers)
- Trucking plan: trailer-direct pick preferred; marshalling yard only if planned
- No double-handling unless approved

HOLD if sequence and fabrication order are misaligned.
